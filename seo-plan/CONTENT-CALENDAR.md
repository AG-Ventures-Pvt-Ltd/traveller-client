## Content Strategy Overview

### Wondrr's Unique Content Angles (exploit these)
1. **Aggregator authority** — "Best travel companies for X" posts that competitors cannot write credibly
2. **Safety narrative** — "Safe solo travel India" content (Wondrr already positions on safety)
3. **Community stories** — "Strangers to friends" real traveler experiences
4. **Operator spotlights** — Introduce the 50+ brands; build E-E-A-T by association
5. **Curation editorial** — "This month's top picks" — only a marketplace can do this neutrally

### Content Pillars
| Pillar | % of Content | Purpose |
|--------|-------------|---------|

## E-E-A-T Implementation Per Post

Every blog post must include:
- [ ] Author byline with name + 1-line bio ("avid solo traveler, has visited 12 Indian states")
- [ ] "Last updated" date
- [ ] Sourced facts (government tourism sites, weather data, distance/cost estimates)
- [ ] Internal links to 2-3 relevant trip listings
- [ ] Internal links to 1-2 related blog posts (once library grows)
- [ ] FAQ section (5+ Q&As) with FAQPage JSON-LD schema
- [ ] Image alt text with keyword context
