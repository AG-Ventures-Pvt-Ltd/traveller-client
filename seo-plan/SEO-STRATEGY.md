**Prepared:** June 2026 | **Audit baseline:** 51/100 | **GEO baseline:** 38/100

## Executive Summary

Wondrr is invisible in organic search. The blog has zero posts. The site has no keyword-optimized destination or category pages. All traffic appears to come from Instagram/direct/paid. Competitors JustWravel (970+ posts), WanderOn (150K community, 30% organic), and CaptureATrip (573K community, Shark Tank) have years of content head starts.

**The opportunity is real:** Wondrr's aggregator/marketplace model is a structural SEO advantage no competitor has. A platform curating 50+ operators can write "best group travel companies India" content with genuine authority. Single-operator brands cannot.

**The strategy:** Fix remaining technical issues (2 weeks) → publish 24 blog posts in 3 months → build destination hub pages → earn backlinks via 10 pre-written guest posts → compound to 100+ posts and top-10 rankings within 12 months.

## On-Page SEO Standards (apply to every page)

### Title Tag Formula
- Homepage: `[Primary KW] | [Brand Descriptor] — Wondrr`
- Destination: `[Destination] Group Trips | Book Verified Tours — Wondrr`
- Blog: `[Primary KW]: [Modifier/Number/Year] | Wondrr`
- Category: `[Trip Type] in India | Curated Group Packages — Wondrr`

### Meta Description Formula (≤160 chars)
- Lead with value prop: "[What you get]"
- Include primary keyword naturally
- Include CTA: "Browse and book now." or "Explore curated options."

### H-tag Rules
- One H1 per page (done ✅ from audit)
- H2s: one per major section, include secondary keywords
- H3s: sub-points, FAQ questions
- Never skip levels (H1 → H3 without H2)

### Internal Linking Rules
- Every blog post → 2-3 trip listing pages (transactional)
- Every blog post → 1-2 related blog posts (once library grows)
- Every destination hub → related trip type pages
- Homepage → top 5 destination hubs + top 3 trip type hubs
- Footer → full destination + trip type grid (see SITE-STRUCTURE.md)

## GEO / AI Search Strategy (score: 38/100)

From the GEO-ANALYSIS.md:

**Already done:** robots.txt allows all major AI crawlers, llms.txt published.

**Critical remaining gaps (Citability = 4/25):**
The biggest AI citation gap is the same as the organic gap — no content to cite.

Once blog posts go live:
1. Structure every post with direct-answer paragraphs (AI Overviews pull from these)
2. Include stats with source citations ("According to India Tourism Statistics 2025...")
3. Use FAQ sections — Perplexity and ChatGPT pull FAQ answers directly
4. Keep definitions crisp: "Group cap of 15 means your trip has a maximum of 15 travelers, ensuring..."
5. Add `SpeakableSpecification` schema to key stat sentences on homepage

**YouTube = highest GEO ROI** per GEO analysis. Create channel and embed videos in blog posts. AI citation tools (Perplexity, ChatGPT) increasingly cite YouTube for experiential content.

## Competitive Response Plan
